  SECTION 1 — EXECUTION PROTOCOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1  SIGNAL GENERATION (1H bar close)
──────────────────────────────────────

  Trigger  : The model is evaluated ONLY at confirmed 1H bar CLOSE.
             "Confirmed" = next 1H bar has opened on the exchange.
             Never evaluate on a bar that is still forming.

  Source   : main.py pipeline on the full historical window up to and
             including the just-closed bar.

  Condition:
    LONG  signal  → P(BUY)  >= 0.55  (buy probability from model)
    SHORT signal  → P(BUY)  <= 0.45  (sell probability >= 0.55)
    FLAT         → all other cases  (no trade)

  Filters (applied in this order before passing to 15m layer):
    1. BLOCK_LOW_VOL = True  → skip if current 1H ATR/ATR_median < 0.75
    2. No open position already exists on this asset (no pyramiding)
    3. Signal must pass 15m-A filter (Section 1.2)

  Reference price: The CLOSE price of the triggering 1H bar.
                   This is the price used by the pullback filter in Step B.

1.2  APPROACH A — MOMENTUM CONSISTENCY FILTER (Hard gate)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Window  : The 4 consecutive 15m bars that open AFTER the 1H signal bar closes.
            i.e., bars at T+0m, T+15m, T+30m, T+45m where T = 1H signal close time.

  Rule    : Count bars where (close > open) for LONG, (close < open) for SHORT.
            If aligned_count < 2  →  CANCEL the trade entirely.
            If aligned_count >= 2 →  Proceed to Step B.

  Timing  : Decision is made at T+60m (i.e., when the 4th 15m bar closes).
             No early exit from the A window.

  Fallback: If 15m data is unavailable (data feed outage) → SKIP the trade.
            Do not fall back to baseline entry when A cannot be evaluated.
            Reason: the A filter is responsible for the majority of the
            system's OOS improvement; bypassing it removes the edge.

1.3  APPROACH B — PULLBACK ENTRY (Price improvement)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Starts  : Immediately after A confirmation (T+60m from signal).
  Window  : Up to 4 additional 15m bars (T+60m to T+120m = bars 5-8).

  Mechanism (state machine):
    Phase 0 — Waiting for pullback:
      LONG  : Any 15m bar where close < 1H_reference_price  → Phase 1
      SHORT : Any 15m bar where close > 1H_reference_price  → Phase 1

    Phase 1 — Waiting for resumption:
      LONG  : Next 15m bar where close > open  → ENTRY at that bar's close
      SHORT : Next 15m bar where close < open  → ENTRY at that bar's close

  Fallback: If no pullback+resumption pattern completes within bars 5-8
            → enter at the CLOSE of bar 8 at the 1H reference price.
            The trade is always taken once A passes. No trades are dropped.

  Entry price (pre-slippage):
    Pullback found : close price of the resumption bar
    Fallback       : close price of the 1H signal bar (reference price)

1.4  FILL PRICE (Slippage model)
──────────────────────────────────

  Entry (market order, taker):
    LONG  fill = entry_price_pre_slip * (1 + 0.0002)   [+2 bps]
    SHORT fill = entry_price_pre_slip * (1 - 0.0002)   [-2 bps]

  Stop loss (market order, adverse fill):
    LONG  fill = stop_price * (1 - 0.0002)
    SHORT fill = stop_price * (1 + 0.0002)

  Take profit (limit order, exact fill):
    Both directions: fill = tp_price  (no slippage)

  Time exit (market order at bar close):
    LONG  fill = bar_close * (1 - 0.0002)
    SHORT fill = bar_close * (1 + 0.0002)

  Commission: 0.10% round-trip, deducted from net P&L post-exit.
    commission = (entry_fill + exit_fill) * position_size_in_units * 0.001

1.5  STOP LOSS AND TAKE PROFIT  (SCALED EXIT, v2)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ATR used  : 14-bar ATR computed on 1H bars, taken at the time of the
              1H signal close (NOT recalculated at 15m entry time).

  Initial SL at entry (= 1R = full risk unit):
    LONG  : stop_price = entry_fill - 1.5 * ATR_1H
    SHORT : stop_price = entry_fill + 1.5 * ATR_1H

  Three partial take-profit levels (defined in R-units off entry):
    sl_dist = |entry_fill - stop_price|   (= 1.5 * ATR_1H = 1R)

    LONG:
      tp1_price = entry_fill + 0.65 * sl_dist  → close 50% of position
      tp2_price = entry_fill + 1.00 * sl_dist  → close 25%
      tp3_price = entry_fill + 1.67 * sl_dist  → close remaining 25%

    SHORT (mirrored):
      tp1_price = entry_fill - 0.65 * sl_dist  → close 50%
      tp2_price = entry_fill - 1.00 * sl_dist  → close 25%
      tp3_price = entry_fill - 1.67 * sl_dist  → close remaining 25%

  Rules:
    - SL and all three TPs are FIXED at entry. No trailing stop.
    - When TP1 fires: stop is moved to BREAKEVEN (entry_fill) on remainder.
      This is the BE stop — protects the unrealized portion from going back
      to original SL.
    - TP1, TP2, TP3 are LIMIT orders (no slippage on fill).
    - SL and BE fills incur taker slippage (Section 1.4).
    - If price gaps through SL or BE: fill at first available price; note
      "gap stop" in Notes.
    - R:R per trade = 1.667 by construction (TP3 vs SL).

  Source of truth: parameters live in config.py (SCALED_TP1_R, SCALED_TP2_R,
  SCALED_TP3_R, SCALED_TP1_FRAC, SCALED_TP2_FRAC, SCALED_TP3_FRAC) and are
  mirrored in paper_trading/config_live.py. backtest/engine.py implements
  the same state machine natively when BACKTEST_EXIT_MODE = "scaled".

1.6  EXIT RULES  (priority order, checked each 1H bar)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  State machine: position is in one of two phases:
    PRE_TP1   — full position open; TP1 has not fired yet
    POST_TP1  — TP1 has fired in a PRIOR bar; partial remainder is open and
                protected by a BE stop at entry price

  Priority within a bar:

  PHASE = PRE_TP1:
    1. SL hit (LONG: low <= stop_price | SHORT: high >= stop_price)
       → close full position at stop fill price, reason = "stop"
    2. TP1 hit (LONG: high >= tp1_price | SHORT: low <= tp1_price)
       → close 50% at tp1_price (limit, no slip)
       → state moves to POST_TP1
       → SAME BAR: also check TP2 and TP3 for cascading partial close
    3. Time exit (24 1H bars elapsed)
       → close full remaining position at bar close + slippage

  PHASE = POST_TP1:
    1. BE stop hit (LONG: low <= entry | SHORT: high >= entry)
       → close remaining fraction at BE fill price, reason = "stop"
       BE STOP IS PRIORITY 1 IN THIS PHASE (replaces the original SL).
    2. TP2 hit (only if not yet hit)
       → close 25% at tp2_price
    3. TP3 hit (LONG: high >= tp3_price | SHORT: low <= tp3_price)
       → close remaining 25% at tp3_price, reason = "tp", trade complete
    4. Time exit (24 1H bars elapsed)
       → close remaining fraction at bar close + slippage

  Same-bar conflicts (conservative ordering, matches backtest engine):
    - In PRE_TP1: if both SL and TP1 touched → assume SL filled first.
    - In POST_TP1: if both BE and TP2/TP3 touched → assume BE filled first.
    - If TP1 fires THIS bar, BE is NOT checked the same bar (only starting
      next bar). This is intentional and mirrors paper_trading exactly.

1.7  POSITION SIZING
──────────────────────

  Position value   = current_equity * 0.10  (10% of equity, single source of
                                              truth: config.POSITION_SIZE_PCT)
  Total units      = position_value / entry_fill_price
  Dollar risk (1R) = total_units * |entry_fill - stop_price|  (full-position risk)

  Partial close arithmetic (scaled exit):
    TP1 fires: close 50% of total_units at tp1_price
    TP2 fires: close 25% of total_units at tp2_price
    TP3 fires: close 25% of total_units at tp3_price
    BE  fires: close current remaining_fraction at entry_fill (BE level)

  Each partial generates its own commission + slippage cost. Net realized PnL
  per trade = sum of all partial closes minus all costs.

  No pyramiding: one open position per asset at all times.
  If a new signal fires on an asset while already in a trade on that asset,
  the new signal is IGNORED.

1.8  MULTI-ASSET CONCURRENT POSITIONS
───────────────────────────────────────

  Up to 4 simultaneous positions allowed (one per asset: AVAX, ADA, SOL, XRP).
  Each position uses 10% of TOTAL current equity at time of entry.
  Maximum concurrent exposure = 40% of equity.
  Positions are independent: no correlation adjustment.

1.9  WHAT IS FORBIDDEN
─────────────────────────

  - Early exit before SL / TP1 / TP2 / TP3 / BE / time trigger
  - Moving SL, BE, TP1, TP2, or TP3 after entry for any reason
  - Adding to a winning or losing position
  - Closing the remainder manually after TP1 fires (BE stop manages it)
  - Skipping a signal that passed A+B because you "don't like the setup"
  - Taking a trade that failed the A filter
  - Any discretionary override of any kind

  For each 1H bar while in trade, record the bar high (for MFE on long)
  and bar low (for MAE on long). Take the max/min across the trade duration.
  MFE and MAE must be populated at trade close, not real-time.
  Do not use 15m bars for MFE/MAE — 1H bars only (consistent with backtest).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1  SCHEDULE
───────────────

  The 1H model fires at every 1H bar close (00:00, 01:00, 02:00 ... 23:00 UTC).
  You must be available to check at the top of each hour OR have an automated
  pipeline that generates signals and alerts you.

  Minimum viable approach (manual):
    Set a repeating hourly alert.
    At each alert, run the signal check (Step 4.2).
    If no signal: 60 seconds of work, return to other activity.
    If signal: switch to 15m monitoring for the next 2 hours (Step 4.3).

  If you cannot monitor every hour:
    Acceptable gaps: up to 2 consecutive hours without checking.
    Rule: if you miss the check window for a signal, DO NOT enter late.
          A missed signal is a missed signal — do not chase.

4.2  HOURLY SIGNAL CHECK (2–5 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────

  Step 1: At 1H bar close (first few seconds of the new bar), pull confirmed
          OHLCV data for AVAXUSDT, XRPUSDT, BTCUSDT.

  Step 2: Run feature generation and model prediction.
          Fastest: pre-built script that reads new bar, recalculates features,
          outputs P(BUY) for each asset.

  Step 3: For each asset, evaluate:
          - Is P(BUY) >= 0.55? → LONG candidate
          - Is P(BUY) <= 0.45? → SHORT candidate
          - Else: FLAT, move on

  Step 4: For any candidate signal:
          - Check: is there already an open position on this asset? If yes: SKIP
          - Check: is ATR/ATR_median >= 0.75? If no (low vol): SKIP, log reason
          - If candidate survives: begin 15m monitoring (Step 4.3)

  Step 5: For any OPEN positions:
          - Check if SL or TP was breached during this 1H bar
          - Check if bar 24 has been reached (time exit)
          - If exit triggered: record exit in trade log immediately

4.3  15m MONITORING (when signal detected)
────────────────────────────────────────────

  Context: You have a signal at time T (1H bar close). You now monitor 15m bars.

  T+0m to T+60m — Approach A window (bars 1–4):
    Watch each 15m bar close.
    Count aligned bars (close > open for long; close < open for short).
    At T+60m:
      aligned < 2 → CANCEL. Log: trade_id, symbol, direction, a_filter_result=fail,
                               a_aligned_bars=N. No further action.
      aligned >= 2 → A PASSED. Begin Phase B.

  T+60m to T+120m — Approach B window (bars 5–8):
    Watch for:
      Phase 0: Price crossing reference_price (pullback)
        LONG:  any 15m bar closes below 1H reference → Phase 1
        SHORT: any 15m bar closes above 1H reference → Phase 1
      Phase 1: Resumption bar
        LONG:  next 15m bar closes above its open → ENTER at that close
        SHORT: next 15m bar closes below its open → ENTER at that close

    If bar 8 (T+120m) closes without pullback+resumption pattern completing:
      FALLBACK: Enter at the close of bar 8 using 1H reference price.

  At entry: immediately record all entry fields in trade log.
            Set alerts for SL, TP, and bar-24 time exit on Bybit paper account.

4.4  TRADE MANAGEMENT (while in position)
────────────────────────────────────────────

  Check at every 1H bar close (same as signal check):
    1. Read bar high and low
    2. Update mfe_price (max(current_mfe, bar_high) for long)
    3. Update mae_price (min(current_mae, bar_low)  for long)
    4. Check exit triggers in the correct phase priority:
         PRE_TP1 phase  : SL > TP1 > Time
         POST_TP1 phase : BE > TP2 > TP3 > Time
    5. Record any partial fill (TP1, TP2, TP3, BE) with its own timestamp,
       fill price, and partial PnL. Final trade close is the LAST partial.
    6. After TP1 fires: ensure Bybit conditional stop is moved to BE (entry).
    7. Check bar count: if bar 24 with remainder open, time-exit remainder.

  Bybit paper account setup:
    - 3 limit orders pre-placed at TP1, TP2, TP3 (closes 50%/25%/25%).
    - 1 conditional stop at SL price.
    - After TP1 fills: cancel old SL conditional and place new one at BE.
    - After TP2 fills: nothing to change.
    - After TP3 fills or BE fills: cancel any remaining orders.

  The manual log is the primary record; Bybit fills are the actual execution.

4.5  END-OF-DAY REVIEW (15–20 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────

  Perform once per day at a fixed time (e.g., 00:00 UTC):

  1. Verify trade log: all opened and closed trades from the day are recorded.
  2. Reconcile with Bybit paper account: fills match your log.
  3. Update running metrics (Section 3.1–3.2). Record in a daily summary row.
  4. Flag any anomalies:
     - Signal fired but no entry was taken → log reason
     - Entry taken without signal → this should never happen; investigate
     - A filter pass rate < 60% or > 90% → possible model drift
  5. Record open positions: current P&L (unrealized), bars held, current MAE.

4.6  WEEKLY REVIEW (30–45 minutes)
─────────────────────────────────────

  Metrics to compute:
  - PF per asset (separate) — target >= 1.45 (live-discounted)
  - Combined PF — target >= 1.55
  - WR per asset — expected ~70-75% (driven by TP1+BE wins)
  - A-filter pass rate vs backtest expectation (77%)
  - Pullback rate vs backtest expectation (~55%)
  - TP1 hit rate (expect ~70%), TP3 hit rate (expect ~27%)
  - BE-stop rate (expect ~40-45% of trades trigger BE after TP1)
  - Pure stop rate (expect ~25-28%)
  - R distribution: plot histogram (expect bimodal peak at +0.325R and -1R)
  - MFE fill rate: MEAN(mfe_r) / 1.667

  Decision rule:
  - If after 4 weeks, any single asset has PF < 1.00 with >= 15 trades:
    PAUSE that asset. Continue on the other three.
  - If combined PF < 1.10 after 4 weeks: PAUSE all assets. Run Section 6 diagnosis.

  SECTION 6 — DETECTING OVERFITTING vs REAL EDGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1  WHAT OVERFITTING LOOKS LIKE IN LIVE TRADING
───────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Pattern 1: Metric collapse across all assets simultaneously
    - All 4 assets show PF < 1.10 at the same time, in the same market period.
    - Interpretation: The model fitted a 2022-2025 macro regime that no longer
      exists. The 1H RF features are no longer predictive.
    - Distinguishing signal: A-filter pass rate stays normal (77+/-5%) but
      trades that pass A still lose. The A filter captures the wrong pattern.

  Pattern 2: A-filter does not differentiate
    - Trades that FAIL A (if you were tracking them hypothetically) would have
      a PF similar to trades that PASS A.
    - A-filter pass rate is wildly different from 77% baseline.
    - Interpretation: 15m momentum is not correlated with 1H signal quality
      in current market conditions. The A filter's design assumption broke.

  Pattern 3: Entry price improvement evaporates
    - Pullback entries show WORSE outcomes than fallback entries.
    - Interpretation: The pullback pattern (counter-move then resumption) is
      not present in current market microstructure. The B approach was tuned
      on a historical pullback rate that no longer occurs.

  Pattern 4: Win rate below 55% persistently (scaled exit)
    - Scaled exit's WR baseline is ~74% (TP1+BE = small win, pure stop = loss).
    - Win rate < 55% means TP1 hit rate dropped below 50% — signal quality
      collapsed. Pure-stop rate spiked above 45%.
    - Interpretation: Either signal direction quality collapsed, or 15m
      microstructure makes TP1=0.65R unreachable in current vol regime.
    - Distinguishing test: check TP1 hit rate directly. If TP1 < 50%, model
      signal is broken. If TP1 ~70% but BE-stops > 55%, market is choppy
      (TP1 reaches, then retraces). Different remedies.

  Pattern 5: MFE fill rate < 40%
    - Price reaches less than 40% of the TP distance before reversing.
    - Interpretation: The 24H forward return window assumption is wrong for
      the current period. 24H moves are not trending enough for 2.5×ATR TP.

  Pattern 6: Strong backtest but weak paper trading — timing mismatch
    - All backtest metrics look good, but paper trades show large slippage.
    - If MEAN(entry_price_fill / entry_price_raw - 1) > 0.10%:
      → You are entering on bar close at a worse price than the model assumes.
      → This is a data latency issue, not a model issue.

6.2  WHAT REAL EDGE LOOKS LIKE
─────────────────────────────────

  Confirmation signals (all are needed together, not any single one):

  Observation                        Expected range      Confirms
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  A-filter pass rate                 72-82%              Model generating quality signals
  Pullback rate                      48-68%              15m microstructure intact
  Win rate                           65-78%              TP1+BE wins dominate
  TP1 hit rate                       65-75%              Scaled-exit core engine working
  Pure-stop rate                     22-32%              Inverse of TP1 hit rate
  BE-stop rate (post-TP1)            40-55%              Expected vs backtest ~43%
  Positive expectancy per trade      > 0                 Edge survives costs
  PF consistent across assets        >= 1.4 on 3/4       Edge is not asset-specific
  PF consistent across time          No single month     Edge is not time-specific
                                     below 1.00
  MFE fill rate                      > 50%               Price moves toward TP (relaxed
                                                          since TP1 fires earlier)
  R distribution: bimodal            Peak at +0.325R     TP1+BE wins + pure stops
                                     and -1.00R
  WR consistent between long/short   Within 10% of each  Not directional bias only

6.3  STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE CHECK
──────────────────────────────────────

  At 40 trades you can already get decent confidence. Scaled exit's high WR
  (~74% expected) makes binomial tests converge faster than v1's 44% WR.

  Binomial test on win rate (null = breakeven WR for the realized R profile):
    Realized R profile is asymmetric: avg win ~+0.45R, avg loss ~-1.00R
    Breakeven WR_be = 1.00 / (1.00 + 0.45) = 69%
    With 40 trades and observed WR >= 75%: reject null at ~80% confidence.
    With 60 trades and observed WR >= 73%: reject null at ~90% confidence.
    ~100 trades for 95% confidence at WR=74%.

  Practical threshold instead of p-values:
    After 40 trades: if avg R > 0.10 and PF > 1.30 -> continue, edge present.
    After 60 trades: if avg R > 0.15 and PF > 1.40 -> promote conservatively.
    After 100 trades: use standard t-test on R distribution.

  Formula for 95% CI on expectancy:
    CI = mean_R +/- (1.96 * std_R / sqrt(N))
    If lower bound of CI > 0 after N trades -> statistically significant edge.

6.4  DIAGNOSTIC DECISION TREE
───────────────────────────────

  Paper trading PF < 1.30 after 40+ trades
  |
  +-- A-filter pass rate abnormal (< 68% or > 88%)?
  |    YES -> 15m data quality issue or model not running correctly.
  |           Check signal generation pipeline for bugs. Not an edge problem.
  |
  +-- All 4 assets down simultaneously in the same calendar period?
  |    YES -> Macro regime shift. The model trained on 2022-2025 does not
  |           generalize to current macro. Retrain on recent data.
  |           Add 6 months of new 1H data. Re-run walk-forward.
  |
  +-- One asset down, three stable?
  |    YES -> Asset-specific issue. Pause that asset. Check for structural
  |           change (volume, liquidity, listing/delisting, correlation change).
  |
  +-- TP1 hit rate < 55%?
  |    YES -> Signal direction quality dropped, OR microstructure changed so
  |           that 0.65R targets are now too far. Raise BUY_PROB_THRESHOLD
  |           to 0.57 in backtest first. Do not change live without WF check.
  |
  +-- TP1 hit rate ~70% but BE-stop rate > 60%?
  |    YES -> Choppy market: price hits TP1 then retraces past entry too often.
  |           Consider lowering TP1 toward 0.50R OR widening BE to +0.10R offset.
  |           Both are backtest-only changes; do NOT modify live without WF.
  |
  +-- MFE fill rate < 40% (post-TP1)?
  |    YES -> TP3 ambitious for current regime; many TP1-hit trades never
  |           reach TP3. Acceptable as long as edge is positive (scaled exit
  |           tolerates this). Consider lowering TP3 toward 1.40R in backtest.
  |
  +-- Pure-stop rate > 38%?
  |    YES -> Entries too early or in wrong direction. Same remedy as low
  |           TP1 hit rate: raise BUY_PROB_THRESHOLD or revisit 15m-A filter.
  │
  └─ None of the above?
       → Statistical variance. Continue trading. At 30 trades you do not
         have enough data to distinguish bad luck from bad model.
         Minimum: wait until 50 trades before any model change.

6.5  WHAT NOT TO DO
─────────────────────

  - Do not re-optimize parameters because paper trading is disappointing.
    Changing parameters after seeing results is classic overfitting.

  - Do not add a new filter because you see a pattern in 10 trades.
    Any pattern in < 50 trades is noise.

  - Do not change SL, TP1, TP2, TP3, or BE level because a few trades
    "almost" reached them. The scaled-exit parameters were chosen via WF
    sweep — do not chase short-term anecdotes.

  - Do not switch from 15m-AB to baseline because A is filtering too much.
    The A filter's value is demonstrated OOS. Do not remove it based on
    short-term results.

  - Do not skip the BE stop manually because "this trade looks like it
    will continue running". The BE stop is part of the edge construction;
    skipping it = manually re-running an experiment with different rules.

  - Do not close TP3 remainder early because the trade is taking too long.
    Time exit handles that automatically at bar 24.

  - Do not compare your paper PF to the full in-sample backtest PF.
    The correct comparison is the 3-year native WF PF (1.85 combined).
    Live target after discount is ~1.45-1.55. Expect drift in either
    direction in the first 40 trades.
